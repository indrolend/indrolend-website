# Journal Entries

## Entry 1
### March 17, 2025

"Branding is perception. Perception can be guided."

People treat branding like a logo, a color scheme, an aesthetic—something surface-level. But branding is really about shaping how people process you. It's psychology, not design.

"Music is the product. Marketing is the art."

Marketing isn't just about selling something. It's world-building. When done right, people don't feel like they're being sold to. They feel like they found something they weren't supposed to.

"I don't sit around trying to make something perfect. I build a system and let it move."

The best ideas happen in motion. You set up the structure, you tweak, you adapt. If you over-control the process, you kill it before it breathes.

"AI doesn't know the truth. It only knows what it finds. So I'm making sure it finds the right things."

Google is already trying to define me, pulling from scattered data—TikTok hashtags, SoundCloud uploads, a new Instagram. I'm feeding it information on purpose. Watching what it does with it.

"The mistake people make is thinking AI is structured. It's not. It's chaos trying to make sense of fragments. So I'm making sure the right fragments are there."

Most people don't realize that AI is constantly rewriting the internet's perception of them. If you understand that, you can shape it instead of letting it shape you.

"The best art isn't consumed. It's discovered."

I don't want my music to be something that's fed to people through an algorithm. I want it to feel like something you stumble upon, something that makes you stop and wonder if you were supposed to find it.

"People don't understand the art of leaking. Of keeping people hungry. Of making them feel like they only got a piece of something bigger."

You don't give people everything at once. That's bad business. People think they want the whole thing, but they don't. If you give them everything, there's nothing left to uncover.

"Scarcity creates demand. Don't overfeed."

People crave what they can't have. If you give them too much, too soon, you take away the chase.

"Some people think marketing is selling. But real marketing is world-building. When you get it right, people sell it to themselves."

Marketing should make people feel like they're part of something. The second they believe it belongs to them, they'll spread it on their own.

"I've been running the guerrilla marketing angle—stickers, cryptic branding, immersion. I don't want people to just listen to my music, I want them to find it."

There's something different about discovering a sticker in the wild and having to search for what it means. It turns passive listeners into engaged ones.

"I'm playing the long game. I just don't know if I have the patience for it."

I know I need to build steadily, but I also want things to move. That's the constant tension: speed vs. quality, output vs. impact.

"People always ask about the end goal. The second you define it, you shrink it."

If you lock yourself into one trajectory, you limit what it can become. I'm leaving space for things to evolve.

## Entry 2
### February 7, 2026

**On value and price**

"Value only exists during exchange… people try to gauge value as something concrete, like price."

"An oil change is $80, then $100, then it used to be $60 or $40… and yet people treat price like truth."

"Value is tied very closely to self-image or self-worth… people point at numbers to remind themselves of who they are."

**On identity being shaped externally**

"An individual person is not removed from being affected by forces outside of them — social perception, environment, circumstance."

"There's a certain level of uncontrollability… so we spend our time wrangling together whatever understanding we can."

**On being misunderstood**

"I've struggled with being misunderstood, or being treated like things I wasn't."

"It becomes a nature versus nurture thing — the way we affect our environment, and the way it affects us."

**On the path of least resistance**

"Systems take the path of least resistance, even if that path is energetically expensive."

"If someone feels insecure, they'll work harder to point at things that prove their value… or they'll bring others down… or lower their standards."

**On avoidance and responsibility**

"Everybody avoids responsibility to a certain degree."

"Part of life is never really knowing where you end and another person begins."

**On miscommunication**

"Everybody misspeaks."

"Imagine being taken for all of your misspoken words — what would you even be after that?"

**On assumption as a survival mechanism**

"In order to keep the world running, we assume things are as they are."

"Trying to overcorrect for lack of perception doesn't guarantee better outcomes."

**On frustration and resentment**

"I feel like a lot of people are subconsciously very frustrated… they hate the person their world is making them into."

"Other people kind of suck sometimes, honestly."

**On opinion as reaction**

"Everybody has an opinion, because an opinion is reactionary."

"When all people have is opinions, they act like what they should be — not what they want."

**On social fear**

"People are embarrassed about things they don't have to be embarrassed about."

"They're afraid of introducing partners to family… afraid of how they'll be perceived."

**On compartmentalized identity**

"People won't be their crazy selves around calm friends."

"They won't be emotional around people they want to impress."

**On how identity actually forms**

"Self-identity is formed across multiple experiences — some internal, some completely outside your control."

"I feel like it's way harder to be yourself than it should be."

**On determinism**

"People are too deterministic about what life is, or what other people are."

"In attempting to have an original thought, you're met with abrasiveness from somewhere you can't describe."

**On internalized social pressure**

"Part of it is other people's opinions.
Part of it is opinions implanted in your brain.
Part of it is not wanting to be unaccepted."

**On life not being conceptual**

"This is all conceptual."

"Life is not the ideas we have about it… life is not beliefs."

**On self-questioning**

"Your attention turns back on yourself and you start asking:
'Why do I feel this way?'
'Why do people act like this?'
'Am I doing the right thing?'"

**On accepting limitation**

"It's easy to accept even your own unacceptance."

"You can believe you're not good at something and then never pursue it — and that belief becomes true."

**On failure and success**

"It's impossible to not fail."

"Success requires failure because you're attempting things that haven't been done, or are rarely done."

**On caring and not caring**

"I try to not give a fuck, but give a fuck at the same time."

"That feels like the only fertile environment where things exist."

**On exchange and lack**

"Society runs on perpetual exchange."

"Exchange implies lack, and the implied resolution of receiving."

**On scale and entropy**

"If there were two people on Earth, life would be simple."

"As more people are introduced, variability increases."

**On systems and artifacts**

"A lot of things are systems."

"Some parts of society are artifacts that no longer serve their purpose."

**On technology as interface**

"We use society like an interface."

"A customer service worker is an interface for getting what you want."

**On technological evolution**

"Phones went from landlines to rotary to glass rectangles."

"Behavior shapes technology, and technology reshapes behavior."

**On static knowledge being a lie**

"These comparisons assume knowledge is static."

"Human knowledge hasn't stopped evolving."

**On false certainty**

"People called Native Americans 'Indians' for generations because of a mistake."

"That assumption just kept propagating."

**On living with provisional understanding**

"We live our lives based on things we assume we understand."

"That's one of the main paradoxes in life."
